White Star Line · Southampton to New York · April 1912

Titanic Voyage

Walk the boat deck at sunset on the last evening, stand at the foot of the Grand Staircase, then stand on the bow at 11:39 pm as the iceberg looms out of the dark.

VR goggles: pick a chapter first, then press Goggles, slide your phone into the headset and turn your head to look around. Tap the screen (or the headset button) to start or stop walking where you look; hold for a second to jump to the next chapter. A VR headset such as a Quest gets an Enter VR button: left stick walks, right stick turns, A or X changes chapter, B or Y switches to the crow’s nest. Voice control: turn it on, then say “walk”, “stop”, “turn left”, “turn right”, “turn around”, “back up”, “next chapter”, “replay” or “crow’s nest”. It listens through whichever microphone your phone is using, including Bluetooth headphones.

Runs in any modern browser; a recent phone or a computer with decent graphics looks best. On a phone: drag to look, use the round pad to move. On a computer: drag with the mouse, WASD or arrow keys to move. In Chapter III the story plays out in real time; Replay starts it again. Human figures are built on the MakeHuman base mesh (CC0).
